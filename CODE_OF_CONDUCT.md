# Code of conduct

Be respectful, specific, and constructive.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, and publishing another person's
private information are not acceptable in project spaces. Technical disagreement is welcome;
personal attacks are not.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation when necessary
to protect contributors and users. Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's
maintainer contact or private security-reporting channel rather than escalating them in public.
